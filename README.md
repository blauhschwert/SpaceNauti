### The Game

1. Minimal & punchy
Black holes are tearing through the cosmos. Dodge, survive, and outmaneuver the collapsing space around you in this fast-paced arcade survival game.
2. Atmospheric & mysterious
Space is breaking apart. Black holes appear without warning, devouring stars, planets—even you. Navigate the void, adapt to shifting gravity, and last as long as you can.
3. Action arcade🎮
Chaos erupts as black holes spawn across the galaxy. Use quick reflexes, warp boosters, and smart positioning to escape their pull and rack up the highest score.
4. Sci-fi adventure👾👽
The universe is unstable. Black holes open randomly, reshaping the battlefield. Explore, upgrade your ship, and survive the gravitational apocalypse.
